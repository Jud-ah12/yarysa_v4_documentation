# Records Module

The **Records Module**, often utilized by staff also known as **Receptionists**, serves as the primary administrative gateway and data management center for the entire Yarysa system. This critical module governs the initial patient-system interactions and ensures the smooth flow of administrative tasks. Its primary functionalities revolve around four core areas: managing **Patients** (including registration, updating demographics, and maintaining records); handling **Appointments** (scheduling, rescheduling, and managing the patient queue); tracking **Inpatients** (managing admissions, transfers, and discharges); and detailing **Payment Details**(recording insurance and billing information). Maintaining high data integrity here is crucial, as the information managed by Receptionists forms the foundational administrative and financial data for all subsequent clinical and operational activities within the facility.

### Patients
This  section displays the information about the registed patients and below information of every patient, are the action links.

**-Veiw Details** 

**-Add Payment**

**-Reassign**

which enable the user to perform immediate tasks related to that specific patient record.
![sample image](/Docs-images/records%20image.png)
The Reassign action link, aids the user to assign the patient to a doctor again.The veiw action link is for the receptionist to have a look into the details of the patient, where the receptionist can either edit the details of the patient or delete it. Add payment action link, helps the user (receptionist) to add a payment method to an existing one, or change the payment method of the patient.<!-- The Appointment action link, is used to book appointments for the patient. -->
