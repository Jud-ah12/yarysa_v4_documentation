# Nurses Module
The Nurses Module is the primary interface for clinical staff responsible for direct patient care, acting as the system's central hub for monitoring, recording, and updating patient health status. This module ensures that all nursing activities are accurately documented and accessible in real-time to the medical team. The module is fundamentally organized around two critical components: the **Assigned Patient** section, which provides nurses with a filtered, prioritized list of patients currently under their specific care; and the **Active Encounters** section, which allows for the quick creation, retrieval, and submission of standardized clinical forms, shift reports, vital signs readings, and procedures performed, thereby maintaining a comprehensive and up-to-date Electronic Patient Record (EPR).

* ## ASSIGNED PATIENT

* ### Veiw Profile
This tab displays the personal information as well as the  medical information of a patient.
![profile](/Docs-images/Veiw%20profile.png)

The **About** tab under this tab shows all personal information relating to the patient.   

The **Patient Record** tab  displays all medical details needed for the nurse to know the about the medical status of the patient. This tab dispays various forms of informations, from charts, to notes to sheets and so on.

The **Request** tab lay out all requests and referrals made by for the patient.

Results from various sources, such as lab tests, medical scans, and imaging reports, are consolidated in the **Results** tab.

Documents like scans, PDFs, and reports that have been uploaded for the patient are found in the **Patient Documents** tab.

<mark>The **Refer Patient** button found at the right hand conner under the veiw profile tab is used to refer patient different doctors.

<mark> The **Add room** button also found at the right hand conner under the veiw profile tab is used to asign a patient to a room when admitted.

* ### Assign to Doctor
This tab is used to assign patients to doctors.

* ### Take Vitals
This is where nurses take information about a patient's vital health status such as Tempreture, pulse, respiratory rate etc.
